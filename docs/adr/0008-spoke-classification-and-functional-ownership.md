# 8. Spoke Classification and Functional Ownership Mapping in CCBA

We decided to formalize the classification of Spoke workspaces within the CCBA Platform into two types: Delivery/Project Spokes (short-term workspaces tied to service contracts) and Functional/R&D Spokes (long-term workspaces tied to internal departments). Under this architecture, the central Hub serves as the digital core of the Integrated Digital Operations Platform (IDOP), managed by the Digital Platform & BIM Technology department. Specialized departments are assigned direct ownership of their respective Functional Spokes—specifically, [Spoke-Legal-Compliance] under the Legal & QA Advisor, [Spoke-BIM-Design-Library] under the BIM Design Department, and [Spoke-Project-Execution] under the BIM Project Department—to develop and maintain standards, checklists, and automation tools. These resources are dynamically proposed and merged into the Hub through Pull Requests (Upstream loop) and synced downstream, ensuring decoupled Git tracking, zero code duplication, and strict role-based quality gates.
